\paragraph{External real-robot substrate.}
We use the official \texttt{franka\_custom} physical-robot trajectories and the released
\texttt{dino\_wm\_droid} checkpoint from \texttt{facebookresearch/jepa-wms}, with the repository
and DINOv2 encoder checked out at the commits recorded in the artifact manifest. We follow the
upstream DINO-WM evaluation configuration: 4 Hz sampling, camera
\texttt{exterior\_image\_2\_left}, two context frames, and two aligned unnormalized
seven-dimensional DROID-format delta-pose actions, deterministically reconstructed by the upstream
\texttt{poses\_to\_diffs} routine from observed robot poses. No transition, image, target, or
model score is simulated.

\paragraph{One-step score.}
For each real transition, the frozen model predicts the next DINO latent from two context frames
and two time-aligned actions reconstructed from consecutive observed poses. The target is the
frozen encoder representation of the actual next frame, and only the final teacher-forced
prediction is scored.
The nonconformity score is latent root-mean-squared error, so the conformal set is a latent ball
centered at the model prediction.

\paragraph{Controlled covariate shift.}
On the finite indexed transition pool, $g_i\in[-1,1]$ is the empirical midrank of the translational
current-context-to-target action magnitude. The tilt never uses the target RGB frame, target latent, or model
residual. For preregistered $\lambda\in\{0,0.5,1,1.5\}$, we set
$P_\lambda(i)\propto\exp(-\lambda g_i)$ and
$Q_\lambda(i)\propto\exp(+\lambda g_i)$. The tilt depends only on the covariate and preserves
common support and the empirical conditional target law exactly. We compare unweighted,
oracle-ratio, estimated-ratio, and ratio-clipped weighted conformal prediction at
$\alpha\in\{0.05,0.1,0.2\}$ across 50 fixed repetitions.
