# Interpretation guardrails for the final paper

Permitted claim: post-hoc one-step marginal coverage under a controlled state-action covariate
shift over an external finite pool of physical-robot transitions.

Do not call the exponential tilt a naturally deployed target policy. Do not claim multi-step
coverage, conditional coverage, latent causal sufficiency, or generalization beyond the indexed
physical trajectories without a separate theorem or analysis. The common-state action-propensity
result is a causal specialization in the theory, not the identification story for this controlled
finite-pool experiment. The upstream delta-pose action covariate is reconstructed from endpoint
robot poses, so it must not be described as a prospectively assigned command.

Report every preregistered cell, exact TV, L1 error, classifier regret, ESS, radius, and the
infinite-radius rate. Do not describe intervals as tight without comparing the reported radius to
an explicit operational scale.
